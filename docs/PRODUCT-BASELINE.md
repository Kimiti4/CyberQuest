# Product Baseline

**Status: foundation baseline; product scope subject to CQ-00 approval.**

## Product

CYBERQUEST combines beginner-friendly digital safety, professional cybersecurity education, story-driven missions and measurable practical competence. Launch direction is Android first with a companion responsive web experience. Initial audience: Kenyan and international English-speaking beginners, students and early-career professionals.

## Learning loop

**Learn → Experience → Defend → Master**

XP, streaks and course completion are engagement signals, not proof of competence. Competency awards require defined evidence and an assessment standard.

## Four experiences

1. Cyber Academy — structured curriculum and lessons.
2. Cyber Defense — defensive decision-making and incident simulations.
3. Cyber Labs — isolated technical practice, deferred beyond the MVP.
4. Cyber Arena — competitions and CTF experiences, deferred.

## MVP scope

- Learner account, profile and dashboard.
- Digital-safety learning path and cybersecurity foundations.
- Declarative, versioned mission engine.
- Phishing Detective as the first mission experience.
- Quizzes, deterministic scoring, explanations and feedback.
- Progress, XP, levels and achievements, with server-authoritative awards.
- Responsive web companion and Android client.
- Offline access to approved content; offline attempts remain untrusted until server validation.

Initial content target: six modules, approximately 30 lessons and 8–12 safe scenario exercises. Content count is a planning target, not an acceptance result.

## First mission sequence

- CQ-001 Suspicious Invoice
- CQ-002 Account Under Attack
- CQ-003 The Dangerous App
- CQ-004 The Weak Password
- CQ-005 The Fake Support Agent
- CQ-006 Unusual Network Activity
- CQ-007 The Compromised Employee
- CQ-008 Secure the Company

All entities, messages, accounts and organisations in exercises are fictional.

## Explicit MVP exclusions

No public penetration-testing targets, unrestricted command execution, learner-controlled code execution, multiplayer range, antivirus, malware removal, real device scanning, endpoint monitoring or professional certification claims. AI is optional and must not be required for core learning or allowed to award competency autonomously.

## Progression

Competency progression is evidence-based. XP may encourage participation but cannot independently unlock a professional competency. Learning content, mission versions, assessment criteria and scoring rules must be versioned.
