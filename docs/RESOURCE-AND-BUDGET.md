## Constraints and exclusions

- This budget assumes substantial founder contribution. It is not sufficient evidence that a fully outsourced native Android, web and backend build can be delivered.
- No current provider price, tax, app-store fee, payment fee or exchange-rate quote has been verified.
- Do not provision paid infrastructure until provider, region, data residency, backup, security and monthly spend caps are reviewed.
- Do not introduce Kubernetes, production cyber ranges, full VMs or paid AI dependencies in the MVP.
- Maintain a monthly cost ledger with budget owner, vendor, service, currency, recurring/one-off classification, cancellation path and actual amount.
- Alert before any recurring commitment exceeds the approved allowance.

## Approval controls

Any recurring service, purchase or budget reallocation requires project-owner approval. Keep invoices and account identifiers out of the public repository. Publish only non-sensitive aggregate budget status if needed.
